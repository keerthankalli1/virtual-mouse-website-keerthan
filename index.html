UNDER THE HOOD

From camera frame
to mouse action.

The application processes camera frames, identifies hand landmarks, interprets gesture rules, and sends the corresponding mouse command.

ⓘ

This project uses MediaPipe's hand-landmark detection and rule-based gesture logic; it does not claim to train a custom AI model.

01

Capture

The webcam supplies live video frames.

02

Detect landmarks

MediaPipe identifies hand points for tracking.

03

Recognize gesture

Finger positions, distances and timing determine the action.

04

Control pointer

PyAutoGUI performs mouse movement, clicks, dragging and scrolling.
